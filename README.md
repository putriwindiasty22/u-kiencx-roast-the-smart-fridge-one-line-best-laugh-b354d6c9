One roast line for the smart fridge:

> My smart fridge shows ads for the food already inside it — my leftovers now have a sponsor and a skip button.

The joke lands on a fresh angle: not a fridge that orders too much milk, but a fridge whose touchscreen treats your own groceries as ad inventory — even last night's leftovers come with a sponsor message and a skip button you never asked for. The sting is the skip button: the one appliance you already paid for now makes you sit through ads to reach food you already own. `main.py` validates the line (one line, 1–160 characters, no leading/trailing whitespace, no control characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
